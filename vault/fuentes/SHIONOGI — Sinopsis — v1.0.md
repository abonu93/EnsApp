## Punti chiave
- **Titolo**: "A Phase 3, multinational, randomized, double-blind, 2-arm study to investigate the efficacy and safety of redasemtide (S-005151) compared with placebo group in adult participants with acute ischemic stroke" (p. 1).
- **Disegno**: fase 3, 1:1, stessi strati del protocollo v2.0; arruolamento entro **24 h** dall'esordio; prima dose entro **60 min** dall'arruolamento; seconda dose 12–36 h dopo (p. 5–6).
- **Numerosità**: 1220 valutabili, circa 1356 arruolati (p. 6).
- **Endpoint**: mRS a 90 giorni; secondari chiave fino a 365 giorni (p. 4).
- **Visite**: G30 (−6/+7), G90 (−7/+14), G180 e G365 (±14); a G90 e G365 telefono ammesso almeno per mRS e AE se la visita in presenza non è possibile (p. 9–11).

## Criteri (testuali)
Riportati in forma abbreviata; differenze sostanziali rispetto al protocollo v2.0:
- Inclusione 3: "Diagnosis of supratentorial ischemic stroke with the main infarction site by computed tomography (CT) scan or magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) at the time of obtaining informed consent." (p. 13) — senza la definizione dei criteri TC.
- Inclusione 4: "Able to be enrolled within 24 hours after the onset of ischemic stroke […]" (p. 13).
- Inclusione 5: "A participant who is judged by the PI not eligible for recanalization thrombolysis or endovascular recanalization therapy (mechanical thrombectomy, local fibrinolytic therapy) for the current stroke. […]" (p. 13).
- Inclusione 6: "Neurologic signs corresponding to a score of 8 to 22 on the NIHSS at baseline (ie, assessed within 1 hour before enrollment) that is not changed by ≥ 4 from the score assessed within at least 1 hour of the baseline measurement." (p. 13).
- Nessun criterio sul peso corporeo (presente nel v2.0, inclusione 8).
- Esclusione 3: "Disability corresponding to an mRS score of ≥ 2 before the onset of ischemic stroke." (p. 14).
- Esclusione 9: "Systolic blood pressure > 220 mm Hg or diastolic blood pressure > 120 mm Hg after antihypertensive treatment." (p. 15) — nel v2.0 "≥".
- Esclusione 16: "Subjects with a known history of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), untreated hepatitis C virus, or hepatitis B virus" (p. 15) — assente nel v2.0.
- Manca l'esclusione dell'infarto cerebellare/troncale (esclusione 5 del v2.0).
