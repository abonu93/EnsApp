## Punti chiave
- **Identificativi**: BI Trial No. 1123-0060; EU CT No. 2025-522542-40-00; UTN U1111-1323-6001 (p. 1). NCT non riportato nel documento.
- **Promotore**: Boehringer Ingelheim International GmbH (p. 1, p. 71). Gestione tramite fornitore FOT e CRO nei paesi partecipanti, non nominati (p. 72). Coordinating Investigator: Bruce CV Campbell, Royal Melbourne Hospital (p. 1).
- **Titolo**: "A Phase III, prospective, randomized, open-label, blinded endpoint assessment (PROBE) to assess efficacy and safety of i.v. tenecteplase vs standard of care in patients with acute ischemic stroke (including wake-up stroke), last known well >4.5 h with imaging evidence of salvageable ischemic tissue" (p. 1).
- **Disegno**: fase III, PROBE, gruppi paralleli, superiorità; randomizzazione 1:1 TNK vs standard of care (SOC) (p. 27). Circa 1325 pazienti (circa 662 per braccio), circa 150 centri in 16 paesi (p. 2–3, p. 29).
- **Randomizzazione**: Big Stick Design, con covariate MT pianificata (sì/no), ora d'esordio nota (sì/no), regione (Cina/Europa/resto del mondo), tipo di imaging (a+b vs c) (p. 64).
- **Intervento**: tenecteplase 0,25 mg/kg (massimo 25 mg), bolo e.v. in 5–10 secondi, dose unica; peso stimato accettato (p. 35); tabella delle dosi per peso, 40 kg → 10,0 mg … ≥100 kg → 25 mg (p. 36–37).
- **Controllo**: SOC senza trombolisi; comprende antiaggregazione con aspirina + inibitore P2Y12, sola aspirina o solo P2Y12, a discrezione dello sperimentatore (p. 28). Il SOC non deve includere trombolitici e.v. né intra-arteriosi (p. 56).
- **Trombectomia**: è esclusa l'intenzione di fare la MT nello stesso ospedale della randomizzazione; i pazienti trasferiti in un altro ospedale per la MT sono eleggibili, sia trasferimenti programmati sia urgenti (p. 29, p. 31).
- **Imaging**: tre vie (a RM perfusione-DWI; b TC-perfusione; c NCCT + angio-TC); la via c è consentita **solo ai centri senza RM-perfusione né TC-perfusione** (p. 30, p. 46). Si prevede che il 25–35% dei pazienti entri tramite la via c (p. 65). Va usato un software automatico (p. 46). Se passano più di 120 min dall'imaging qualificante alla randomizzazione, l'imaging va ripetuto (p. 46). Va sempre documentato l'ASPECTS nelle TC (p. 46).
- **Endpoint primario**: mRS 0–1 a 90 giorni (p. 25), valutato in cieco da un valutatore centrale per telefono (p. 42).
- **Endpoint secondari**: mRS ordinale a 90 giorni; miglioramento neurologico precoce (riduzione del NIHSS a 24 h ≥8 oppure NIHSS 0/1); mRS 0–2 a 90 giorni. Sicurezza: sICH secondo SITS-MOST e secondo ECASS III a 36 h; morte per qualsiasi causa entro 90 giorni (p. 25).
- **Endpoint ulteriori**: mRS a 30 giorni (0–1, ordinale, 0–2); variazione del NIHSS a 24 h; EQ-5D-5L a 90 giorni; SAE emergenti dal trattamento; morte e sICH fatale (SITS-MOST) fino al giorno 7 o alla dimissione; tempo alla morte; sanguinamento sistemico moderato o grave (GUSTO) fino al giorno 7 (p. 26).
- **Analisi ad interim**: al 53% (702 pazienti), regola di O'Brien-Fleming, nessuna analisi di futilità (p. 27, p. 62). Il DMC può raccomandare di limitare la popolazione a 4,5–24 h o di modificare i criteri di imaging (p. 27, p. 72).
- **Esami prima del farmaco**: obbligatoria solo la glicemia capillare (POCT); emocromo e coagulazione solo se il paziente assume anticoagulanti o si sospetta una coagulopatia; test di gravidanza su urine, negativo, nelle donne in età fertile (WOCBP). La PA va portata sotto 185/110 prima dell'inclusione (p. 7).
- **Terapie concomitanti**: braccio TNK, nessun antiaggregante, anticoagulante o altro prodotto che influenzi la coagulazione (compresi trombolitici intra-arteriosi) nelle prime 24 h; eparina e.v. vietata prima, durante e per 24 h dopo il farmaco, e poi solo dopo un secondo imaging senza segni di sanguinamento (p. 40).
- **Raccolta degli eventi avversi**: nessun AE non serio viene raccolto; si raccolgono gli SAE dal consenso alla fine dello studio e i sanguinamenti secondo GUSTO fino a 7 giorni (p. 45, p. 49–50). REP: 7 giorni (p. 18).

## Criteri (testuali)

### Inclusion criteria (§3.3.2, p. 30)
1. Male or female ≥18 years old and at least at the legal age of consent in countries where it is greater than 18 years
2. Signed and dated written informed consent in accordance with ICH-GCP and local legislation prior to admission to the trial. See Section 8.1 for details
3. Acute ischaemic stroke (including wake-up stroke) affecting the supratentorial circulation (anterior [ACA], middle [MCA], and posterior cerebral arteries [PCA]) last known well >4.5 h before time of presumed randomisation
4. Pre-stroke mRS ≤1
5. Imaging eligibility (either a, b or c):
   a) MRI criteria (both must be met)
      - Mismatch Ratio: The area of reduced blood flow at risk on MRI (perfusion lesion) is at least 1.2 times (20%) larger than the area of established brain injury (DWI lesion)
      - Mismatch Volume: The difference between the perfusion lesion and DWI lesion is more than 10 mL

   b) NCCT-CT Perfusion criteria (both must be met)
      - The area of reduced blood flow at risk on CT perfusion (perfusion lesion) is at least 1.2 times (20%) larger than the estimated ischemic core lesion AND lesion on NCCT is not larger than the estimated ischemic core lesion
      - The mismatch volume is more than 10 mL

   c) If neither MRI or CT perfusion are available at the site (both must be met)
      - CTA depicting blood vessel blockage: Presence of a blockage in specific supratentorial circulation brain arteries M1 or M2 segments of the Middle Cerebral Artery (MCA)
      - Small lesion on NCCT: ASPECTS ≥7 with M1 MCA occlusions; for M2 MCA occlusions, a maximum of 1 ASPECTS regions involved distal to the occlusion (ASPECTS 9-10)

### Exclusion criteria (§3.3.3, p. 31–33)
1. Intention to proceed to MT at the same site (hospital) of randomisation. (p. 31)

*Imaging Exclusion criteria*

2. Occlusion of the internal carotid artery (ICA) (p. 31)
3. High-risk patients (increased risk of thrombolysis related hemorrhage), defined as: (p. 31)
   a. Large brain infarcts:
      i. On MRI: area of brain injury by DWI ≥70 mL
      ii. On CT perfusion: estimated ischemic core ≥70 mL
      iii. On NCCT: ASPECTS score between 0-6
   b. Severely injured brain tissue:
      i. On NCCT: Patients whose brain lesion shows intense hypo-attenuation when compared to the normal white matter on the opposite side
4. Any intracranial hemorrhage detected on NCCT or MRI scans (p. 31)
5. Contra-indication to contrast brain imaging with CT and MRI (p. 31)

*Clinical Exclusion criteria*

6. Severe stroke as assessed clinically (NIHSS > 25) (p. 31)
7. Non-disabling minor stroke symptoms (NIHSS ≤5), or rapidly improving symptoms at the discretion of the investigator (p. 31)
8. Imaging or clinical findings not indicative of acute ischemic stroke or suggesting stroke older than 72 h (p. 31)
9. Patients scheduled to receive i.v. thrombolysis as standard of care (p. 31)
10. Other known or identified relevant central nervous system damage (i.e., neoplasm, arterial aneurysm and/or arterial/venous malformation, intracranial or spinal surgery) (p. 31)

*Contra-indications to tenecteplase*

11. Significant bleeding disorder (haemorrhagic diathesis) either at present or within the past 6 months (p. 31)
12. Platelet count below 100 000/mm³ (p. 31)
13. Anticoagulant use with following attributes: (p. 32)
    a. If on Vitamin K antagonists, INR >1.7 or prothrombin time >15 seconds;
    b. use of any direct thrombin inhibitors or direct factor Xa inhibitors during the last 48 hours;
    c. any full dose heparin/heparinoid during the last 24 hours or with an aPTT greater than the upper limit of normal
14. Arterial puncture at a non-compressible site or lumbar puncture within 7 days (p. 32)
15. Acute pancreatitis (p. 32)
16. Severe hepatic dysfunction, including hepatic failure, cirrhosis, portal hypertension (oesophageal varices) and active hepatitis (p. 32)
17. Blood glucose <50 mg/dL or >400 mg/dL (<2.8 mM or >22.2 mM). Inclusion of the patient is possible if hypoglycemia has been corrected between screening and randomisation (p. 32)
18. Severe uncontrolled arterial hypertension that cannot be reverted by anti-hypertensive drugs (i.e. systolic BP >185 mmHg, or diastolic BP >110 mmHg) (p. 32)
19. Known severe renal impairment (eGFR <15 ml/min/1.73m2) (p. 32)
20. Active ulcerative gastro-intestinal disease (p. 32)
21. Any history of prior stroke and concomitant diabetes (p. 32)
22. Major surgery, biopsy of a parenchymal organ, recent trauma to the head or significant trauma within the past 2 weeks (p. 32)
23. Prolonged cardiopulmonary resuscitation (>2 minutes) within the past 2 weeks (p. 32)
24. Acute pericarditis and/or subacute bacterial endocarditis (p. 32)
25. Active peptic ulceration (p. 32)
26. Any known history of haemorrhagic stroke (p. 32)
27. Known history of ischaemic stroke or transient ischaemic attack in the preceding 3 months (p. 32)
28. Women who are pregnant or breastfeeding (p. 32)
29. Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the tenecteplase excipients listed, or to gentamicin (a trace residue from the manufacturing process) (p. 32)

*Other Exclusion criteria*

30. Any terminal illness such that patient would not be expected to survive more than 1 year (p. 33)
31. Participants who must or wish to continue the intake of restricted medications (see Section 4.2.2.1) or any drug considered likely to interfere with the safe conduct of the trial (p. 33)
32. Participants not expected to comply with the protocol requirements or not expected to complete the trial as scheduled (e.g. chronic alcohol or drug abuse or any other condition that, in the investigator's opinion, makes the participant an unreliable trial participant) (p. 33)
33. Previous enrolment in this trial (p. 33)
34. Currently enrolled in another investigational device or drug trial, or less than 30 days since ending another investigational device or drug trial(s) or receiving other investigational treatment(s). Participation in purely observational trial(s) is allowed. (p. 33)
35. Any condition not covered by any of the other exclusion criteria which, in the investigator's opinion, might jeopardise the participant's safety or compliance with the protocol (p. 33)
36. Any vulnerable person (defined as: pregnant or breastfeeding women; persons deprived of their liberty; minors; persons that may have insufficient power, intelligence, education, resources, strength, or other needed attributes to protect their own interests), as per local regulation (p. 33)

## Note e anomalie
- **Nome del file fuorviante**: il nome Drive dice "Protocolo y enmienda", ma il documento è il protocollo originale senza emendamenti (§11, p. 83).
- **Finestra superiore di 72 h**: non compare fra i criteri di inclusione (IC3 dice solo ">4.5 h"); deriva dall'esclusione 8, "findings … suggesting stroke older than 72 h" (p. 31). È un giudizio clinico-radiologico, non un limite esplicito sul tempo dall'ultima volta visto bene (LKW).
- **Popolazione modificabile dal DMC**: il DMC può raccomandare di limitare la popolazione da 4,5 a 24 h (p. 72) o di cambiare finestra e criteri di imaging (p. 27).
- **Via c riservata ai centri senza perfusione**: "Only sites without capabilities for neither MRI perfusion nor CT perfusion will be allowed to enroll participants by the NCCT / CTA criterion" (p. 46). È un requisito del centro, non del singolo paziente.
- **Diabete**: l'esclusione 21 riguarda solo l'associazione "prior stroke **and** concomitant diabetes" (p. 32), non il diabete da solo.
- **Dose indicata in modo incoerente**: nel §7 la dose è chiamata "tenecteplase 25 mg (test)" (p. 56–57), mentre altrove è 0,25 mg/kg con massimo di 25 mg (p. 35).
- **Titolo della Tabella 3**: si chiama "Dosage and treatment schedule for the titration phase" (p. 36), ma non esiste una fase di titolazione (bolo unico).
- **NIHSS**: §5.1.1.2 dice "composed of 11 items" (p. 42), mentre la tabella in appendice ne elenca 15 sotto-voci (1a–11) (p. 81–82). È un refuso irrilevante dal punto di vista clinico.
- **Persone vulnerabili e consenso**: l'esclusione 36 esclude le "persons that may have insufficient … attributes to protect their own interests", "as per local regulation" (p. 33). Il §8.1 però ammette il consenso del rappresentante legale o del caregiver (p. 66). L'applicazione ai pazienti afasici o senza capacità di decidere dipende dalla normativa locale; il documento non lo risolve.
- **Stampa**: dalla p. 8 l'intestazione riporta "25 Ju1 2025" (refuso tipografico per "Jul").
- **Dati personali omessi**: il frontespizio contiene recapiti personali (email e telefoni); non sono stati riportati in questa nota.
