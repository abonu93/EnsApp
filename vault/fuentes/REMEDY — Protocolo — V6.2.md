## Punti chiave
- **Titolo**: "Phase 2/3 Adaptive Design, Randomized Double-blind Placebo-controlled Study to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of DM199 for the Treatment of Acute Ischemic Stroke (ReMEDy2 Trial)". Codice DM199-2021-001 (p. 1).
- **Promotore**: DiaMedica Therapeutics Inc. (Minneapolis, USA). **Registro**: IND 151943, NCT05065216, EU CT 2024-513911-28-00 (p. 1).
- **PI nazionali**: Scott Kasner (University of Pennsylvania), Rachel Laursen (Oregon Health Sciences University) (p. 1).
- **Versioni**: 1.0 (26-05-2021), 2.0 (15-10-2021), 3.0 (02-08-2023), 4.0 (02-10-2023), 4.1 (02-11-2023), 5.0 (26-08-2024), 6.0 (14-05-2025), 6.2 (29-10-2025, "[EU Only]"). La 6.1 non è elencata (p. 1).
- **Farmaco**: DM199 (rinvecalinase alfa), callicreina tissutale umana ricombinante (rhKLK1). Agisce tramite bradichinina: vasodilatazione, flusso collaterale verso la penombra (p. 26–27).
- **Popolazione bersaglio**: ictus ischemico di gravità moderata entro 24 h "due to small and medium vessel occlusions", con poche opzioni terapeutiche. Esclusi i pazienti che ricevono o riceveranno trombectomia; i pazienti trombolisati solo se hanno un deficit moderato persistente ≥6 h dopo la fibrinolisi (p. 10, p. 33). La trombolisi e la trombectomia indicate non vanno negate (p. 10).
- **Disegno**: fase 2/3 adattivo, randomizzato 1:1, doppio cieco, controllato con placebo (p. 12, p. 35). Randomizzazione via IRT, stratificata per NIHSS allo screening (5–10 vs 11–15) e fibrinolisi precedente sì/no (p. 46).
- **Numerosità**: 364 pazienti (182 per braccio) per 90% di potenza; parte A con analisi ad interim dopo 200 pazienti a 90 giorni (futilità non vincolante, ricalcolo della numerosità); parte B fino a 528, massimo 728 in totale (p. 15, p. 36, p. 73–74). Circa 100 centri (p. 10).
- **Storia della dose**: dopo 9 pazienti a 1 µg/kg e.v., 3 ipotensioni gravi; arruolamento sospeso il 18-05-2022 e clinical hold dell'FDA. Causa: passaggio a materiali di infusione in PVC, che non adsorbono il farmaco (dose effettiva doppia rispetto a ReMEDy1). Dose e.v. ridotta a 0,5 µg/kg (max 50 µg) (p. 25, p. 31–32, p. 35). I 9 pazienti iniziali sono esclusi dalle popolazioni di analisi (p. 73).
- **Schema di trattamento** (p. 16, p. 41, p. 43–46):
  - e.v. unica di 0,5 µg/kg (max 50 µg) in 50 mL di fisiologica, sacca precondizionata con polisorbato 80; inizio a 17,5 mL/h per 15 min, poi 25 mL/h per 15 min, poi 35 mL/h (facoltativo nei pazienti con esposizione recente ad ACEi);
  - prima s.c. di 3 µg/kg a 2 h (+10 h) dalla fine dell'infusione, poi due volte a settimana fino al giorno 21, **7 dosi s.c. in totale**, a ≥24 h l'una dall'altra;
  - non iniziare se PAS <100 mmHg o PAM <65 mmHg; infusione iniziata **<24 h** dal LKW (Tab. 3, p. 43);
  - stop definitivo se servono vasopressori, o con PAS <90 o PAM <65 **e** segni di ipotensione (p. 45).
- **Somministrazioni s.c. fuori ospedale**: da sanitari qualificati o da un caregiver formato; infermieria domiciliare del promotore ammessa (p. 45–48).
- **Farmaci concomitanti**:
  - ACEi: ultima dose ≥24 h prima dell'infusione e.v. (≥48 h se eGFR <45); da evitare fino al giorno 29 ±1; cambio di antipertensivo a giudizio del medico (p. 48);
  - vietati i farmaci per l'angioedema ereditario e quelli che agiscono sul sistema callicreina-chinina (p. 49);
  - antiaggreganti e anticoagulanti **ammessi** secondo la pratica locale (p. 49).
- **Endpoint primario**: mRS 0–1 a 90 giorni (CMH stratificato per NIHSS e fibrinolisi) (p. 33, p. 75). Secondari: shift del mRS, mRS 0–2, mortalità, NIHSS 0–1, Barthel ≥95, recidiva ischemica a 90 giorni (p. 33–34).
- **AESI**: angioedema facciale, orale o delle vie aeree; ipotensione; flushing; ipotensione ortostatica; reazioni locali nel sito d'iniezione (p. 63).
- **Esami prima della randomizzazione**: test di gravidanza su siero, locale, negativo prima della randomizzazione nelle donne fertili (p. 14, p. 20, p. 61–62). Laboratorio centrale allo screening (ematologia, biochimica con eGFR, coagulazione, urine) (p. 19, p. 61). Solo la PA serve allo screening per i criteri (p. 20, p. 59).
- **NIHSS** "immediately before Randomization" (nota 2 della SoA, p. 20), da valutatori certificati; ammesso via telemedicina (p. 57).
- **Imaging di controllo** richiesto solo per escludere la trasformazione emorragica dopo fibrinolisi o peggioramento (nota 5 della SoA, p. 20).
- **Consenso**: scritto, dal paziente o dal rappresentante legale (LAR) dove la legge lo consente (art. 31 del Reg. UE 536/2014); ri-consenso del paziente appena recupera la capacità (p. 79–80).
- **Durata**: circa 90 giorni per paziente; trattamento dal giorno 1 al 21; follow-up ai giorni 30 e 90 (p. 16).
- **Figura 1** (immagine, p. 18): Parte A (fino a N=200) → R 1:1 DM199/placebo → analisi ad interim → stop per futilità oppure prosecuzione con ricalcolo → Parte B (fino a N=728) → analisi finale.
- **Figura 4** (immagine, p. 40): screening e consenso → R 1:1 → e.v. 0,5 µg/kg → s.c. due volte a settimana fino al giorno 21 → follow-up ai giorni 30 e 90.

## Criteri (testuali)

### 6.1 Inclusion Criteria (p. 37; sinossi p. 12–13)
"Individuals must meet all of the following criteria to be included in the study:"
1. Participant is between 18 and 90 years of age inclusive.
2. Participant weight is 40 kg to 166 kg inclusive.
3. Participant to be randomized and infusion with investigational product initiated within 24 hours of last known normal/AIS stroke onset.
4. Participant has NIHSS ≥5 and ≤15 at approximately the time of randomization. This criterion also applies to participants who meet the following conditions:
   - The participant initially presents with an NIHSS score below 5 but clinically worsens, including cases of progressing stroke/stroke-in-evolution, resulting in a subsequent persistent NIHSS score of ≥5 and ≤15; and
   - Participant meets all other inclusion and exclusion criteria, including repeat brain imaging to rule out hemorrhagic transformation.
5. Participant had a pre-morbid mRS score of 0 to 1 (mRS score prior to AIS) as stated by participant or participant's representative.
6. If participant has received fibrinolytic treatment for AIS within 4.5 hours of last known normal/AIS stroke onset and at least 6 hours after completing fibrinolytic treatment, and the participant meets all of the following criteria:
   - Participant's initial NIHSS score prior to fibrinolytics was ≤15; and
   - At least six hours after fibrinolytics, the participant has NIHSS score of ≥5 and ≤15 with a persistent deficit; and
   - The participant's NIHSS score showed less than a 4-point improvement, or worsened, after receiving fibrinolytics; and
   - Participant meets all other inclusion and exclusion criteria including repeat brain imaging to rule out hemorrhagic transformation. Hemorrhagic transformation is defined as any of the following:
     - PH1 or PH2 type hemorrhage based on Heidelberg Classification
     - Hemorrhage resulting in neurologic deterioration
     - Note: HI1 and HI2 type hemorrhages based on Heidelberg classification are not exclusionary.
7. Participant and/or legally authorized representative is able to provide informed consent.
8. Participant is willing and able to comply with the study protocol, in the Investigator's judgment.

### 6.2 Exclusion Criteria (p. 38–40; sinossi p. 13–15)
"Individuals meeting any of the following criteria at Screening are ineligible to participate in this study:"
1. At screening, or with repeat imaging (see Inclusion 4 and 6), participant has imaging confirmed hemorrhage stroke. (p. 38) — sinossi: "hemorrhagic stroke" (p. 13)
2. Participant has image findings with symptomatic large vessel occlusion at one or more of the following locations: Intracranial carotid I/T/L or M1 segment MCA, vertebral or basilar artery (BA). (p. 38)
3. Participant has large core of established infarction defined as Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score (ASPECTS) 0-5. (p. 38)
4. Participant has or will receive MT for their current AIS. Diagnostic angiogram without proceeding to MT is not exclusionary). (p. 38)
5. Participant has suspected or confirmed extracranial arterial dissection. (p. 38)
6. Participant has imaging findings and symptoms consistent with a brain stem or cerebellar stroke. Posterior cerebral artery strokes without any associated brain stem or cerebellar involvement are allowable. (p. 38)
7. Participant has 2 consecutive recorded measurements of SBP <100 mmHg or MAP <65 mmHg; MAP = DBP + [1/3 (SBP – DBP)] after stroke symptom onset and within 6 hours prior to randomization. (p. 38)
8. Participant is currently prescribed angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor (ACEi) and is unable or unwilling to convert to another antihypertensive pharmacological treatment through Day 29 ±1 day (8 days after last treatment). (p. 38)
9. Participant is currently prescribed an (ACEi), and the last reported dose by the participant or their representative: (p. 38)
   - was taken less than 24 hours prior to the start of IV IP infusion for participants with normal or mild (CKD 1-3a [eGFR ≥ 45 mL/min/1.73 m²]), or
   - was taken less than 48 hours prior to the start of IV IP infusion for participants with significant (CKD 3b or higher [eGFR < 45 mL/min/1.73 m²])
10. Participant has a history of clinically significant allergic reactions such as angioedema or anaphylaxis requiring hospitalization. (p. 38)
11. Participant has a diagnosis or suspected diagnosis of hereditary angioedema (HAE) or is taking or prescribed medications commonly used as prophylaxis/treatment of HAE, such as C1-esterase inhibitors (Cinryze, Berinert, Ruconest, Haegarda), Danazol, kallikrein inhibitors (Ecallantide, Berotralstat, Lanadelumab), Bradykinin B2 Receptor Antagonists (Icatibant), or other medication designed to influence the kallikrein-kinin system. (p. 38)
12. Life expectancy estimated at ≤1 year prior to enrollment. (p. 38)
13. Participant has clinical evidence of an active infection at the time of enrollment requiring parenteral treatment or hospitalization to monitor or manage the infection. NOTE: Treatment of uncomplicated infections with oral antibiotics would not be an exclusion (for example, the treatment of uncomplicated urinary tract infections or sinus infections with oral antibiotics would not be exclusionary). (p. 38–39)
14. Participant has known alpha 1-antitrypsin deficiency (α1-antitrypsin deficiency). (p. 39)
15. Participant is pregnant or nursing. NOTE: Participants who agree to stop nursing may be considered for inclusion at the discretion of the Investigator. (p. 39)
16. Participants of child-bearing potential who do not agree to use medically acceptable contraceptive measures to prevent pregnancy. All participants of childbearing potential (…) must have a negative serum pregnancy test performed locally at screening. (…) participants must use 2 reliable methods (1 per partner is acceptable) of contraception simultaneously while receiving protocol-specified medication and during the study follow-up period. (…) highly effective birth control methods (…) until they have completed the study (after the Day 90 visit). (…) Participants are prohibited from sperm donation. (p. 39; testo integrale con l'elenco dei metodi a p. 39)
17. Participant is currently participating in or has participated in a study using an investigational device or drug or received an investigational drug or investigational use of a licensed drug within 30 days prior to screening. (p. 39)
18. Participant does not have sufficient venous access for infusion of investigational product or blood sampling. (p. 40)
19. Participant is unable or unwilling to comply with protocol requirements, including assessments, tests, and follow-up visits. (p. 40)
20. Participant has any other medical condition (such as hemodialysis) which in the opinion of the Investigator will make participation medically unsafe or interfere with the study results. (p. 40)

## Note e anomalie
- **Indicazione più ampia del criterio 2**: il §7.1 aggiunge "and participant does not have a large vessel occlusion" (p. 41) e l'obiettivo parla di "small and medium vessel occlusions" (p. 10, p. 33). L'esclusione 2 elenca però solo ICA intracranica (I/T/L), M1, vertebrale e basilare, e solo se **sintomatica** (p. 38). Le M2 e le altre occlusioni non elencate non sono escluse dal testo dei criteri.
- **Esclusione 1**: "hemorrhage stroke" nel §6.2 (p. 38) contro "hemorrhagic stroke" nella sinossi (p. 13). Refuso.
- **Esclusione 9 nel §6.2**: mancano le parole "kidney disease" ("normal or mild (CKD 1-3a …)"), presenti nella sinossi (p. 14). Refuso.
- **Monitoraggio dopo l'infusione**: la Tab. 3 dice "5 min (±5 min) after IV infusion is complete" (p. 44); la nota 6 della SoA e il §9.1.5.2 dicono 15 min (±5) (p. 20, p. 59).
- **§8.1.1 incompleto**: "If a participant misses a scheduled SC dose, they should take the missed dose as soon as possible, provided the following conditions have been met:" e poi nessun elenco (p. 50).
- **Rimando errato**: la sinossi rimanda alla "Section 13" per le analisi (p. 17), ma la sezione 13 è la bibliografia (p. 87).
- **ReMEDy1**: il §3.3.3.1 chiama "excellent functional outcomes (NIHSS ≤1)" un esito neurologico (p. 30).
- **NIHSS "comprised of 11 items"** (p. 57): refuso ricorrente (le voci sono 15).
- **Storia delle versioni**: manca la 6.1; la 6.2 è marcata "[EU Only]" (p. 1).
- **Dati personali omessi**: pagine firme (p. 2–3); telefono del promotore non riportato.
