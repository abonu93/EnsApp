## Punti chiave
- **Titolo**: "A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind, Parallel-Group, Placebo-Controlled Study to Demonstrate the Efficacy and Safety of Milvexian, an Oral Factor XIa Inhibitor, for Stroke Prevention after an Acute Ischemic Stroke or High-Risk Transient Ischemic Attack". Acronimo: LIBREXIA-STROKE. Protocollo 70033093STR3001, fase 3 (p. 1).
- **Promotore**: Janssen Research & Development, in collaborazione con Bristol Myers Squibb (co-sviluppo di milvexian, JNJ-70033093 / BMS-986177) (p. 1, p. 9).
- **Registri**: EU CT 2022-501176-26-00; IND 138848 (p. 1). NCT non riportato.
- **Storia del documento**: originale 01-08-2022; Amendment 1 02-11-2022; Amendment 1/EEA-1 03-06-2023; Amendment 2 e 2/EEA-1 10-04-2024 (p. 2).
- **Disegno**: fase 3, multicentrico, randomizzato 1:1, in doppio cieco, controllato con placebo, a gruppi paralleli, guidato dagli eventi, di superiorità (p. 31). Randomizzazione centrale con IWRS, a blocchi per paese, stratificata per terapia antiaggregante prevista (SAPT o DAPT) (p. 46–47).
- **Bracci**: milvexian 25 mg due volte al giorno contro placebo, in aggiunta a SAPT o DAPT a scelta dello sperimentatore. L'ASA va limitato a 75–100 mg/die; le dosi di carico sono ammesse (p. 10, p. 31–32, p. 44–45).
- **Numerosità ed eventi**: circa 15.000 partecipanti per 909 eventi primari (HR 0,8, potenza circa 90%, alfa bilaterale 0,05, sospensione del trattamento del 10% annuo). Arruolamento di circa 40 mesi, durata totale di circa 42 mesi (p. 32, p. 71). Interim a circa 546 eventi, cioè circa il 60% (p. 77).
- **Endpoint primario**: tempo al primo ictus ischemico; gli ictus di tipo indeterminato sono inclusi (p. 29, p. 72).
- **Endpoint secondari**: composito di morte cardiovascolare, IM o ictus ischemico; ictus ischemico nei primi 90 giorni; MAVE, cioè morte CV, IM, ictus ischemico, MALE ed EP o TVP sintomatica (p. 29).
- **Endpoint di sicurezza**: emorragie gravi BARC 3c e 5 (p. 30, p. 74).
- **Finestra**: randomizzazione il prima possibile, **entro 48 h** dall'esordio. Nell'ictus al risveglio le 48 h si contano dall'ultima volta in cui il paziente è stato riferito normale (p. 39–40).
- **Dopo trombolisi o trombectomia** (con o senza stent): randomizzazione **>24 h dopo la fine** del trattamento ed entro 48 h dall'esordio. Servono un neuroimaging post-trattamento senza emorragia, INR ≤1,5 e aPTT ≤1,4 volte l'ULN (p. 31, p. 40, p. 49).
- **Quote di arruolamento**:
  - si punta a circa il 25% di partecipanti non trattati con trombolisi o trombectomia e randomizzati entro 24 h dall'esordio (era il 50% prima dell'Amendment 2, p. 3);
  - si può introdurre un tetto ai randomizzati dopo 24 h (p. 31, p. 39, p. 47);
  - l'**ictus lacunare è ammesso**, con un tetto di circa il 20% (p. 33, p. 39; introdotto dall'Amendment 1, p. 114).
- **Gravità**: ictus con NIHSS ≤7 **alla randomizzazione**; AIT ad alto rischio con ABCD2 ≥6 (p. 31, p. 39–40). Il NIHSS dello screening vale per la randomizzazione se misurato entro 6 h e senza cambiamenti clinici evidenti. L'ABCD2 va confermato alla randomizzazione (p. 16).
- **Prima dose**: "as close to randomization as possible, ideally within 2 hours of randomization". Se il paziente non deglutisce, il farmaco si può disperdere in acqua (anche via sondino) o in purea di mela (p. 44).
- **Esami di eleggibilità**: locali; valgono anche quelli fatti per pratica clinica dopo l'evento indice. AST, ALP e cistatina C non sono richieste allo screening (p. 16, p. 63). I campioni centrali si raccolgono al giorno 1 prima della prima dose (p. 16, p. 64).
- **Eparina profilattica**: chi ha ricevuto UFH o EBPM a dose profilattica per la TVP prima della randomizzazione può entrare, con una sovrapposizione di **3 giorni** con il farmaco in studio. Poi l'anticoagulante profilattico è vietato; si raccomanda la compressione pneumatica intermittente. Dopo il primo ricovero la profilassi anticoagulante è vietata (p. 49–50).
- **Farmaci vietati** (p. 49–50):
  - ASA >100 mg/die per più di 4 settimane;
  - isoniazide;
  - omeprazolo o esomeprazolo insieme al clopidogrel;
  - altri anticoagulanti;
  - inibitori combinati di P-gp e CYP3A4 forti e induttori combinati di P-gp e CYP3A4/5 forti, entro 7 giorni.
  - Gli altri FANS sono ammessi solo in modo temporaneo.
- **Antidoto**: nessun antidoto specifico. Si possono usare rFVIIa, FEIBA e acido tranexamico; protamina, vitamina K, idarucizumab e andexanet non sono attesi efficaci (p. 63).
- **Trombolisi o trombectomia durante lo studio**: per una recidiva non serve ritardarle per l'ultima dose del farmaco (p. 55).
- **Consenso**: ICF firmato prima di qualunque procedura. Se il paziente non può consentire, firma il rappresentante legale (p. 16, p. 36, p. 41, p. 84–85). L'Amendment 1 ha eliminato il testo che permetteva l'arruolamento prima del consenso (p. 114). Il consenso scritto può essere anche elettronico (p. 36).

## Criteri (testuali)

### 5.1 Inclusion Criteria (p. 39–41)
"Each potential participant must satisfy all of the following criteria to be enrolled in the study:"

*Age*
1. ≥40 years of age (p. 39)

*Type of Participant and Disease Characteristics*

2. Ischemic Stroke: a neurological deficit attributable to an acute brain infarction and NIHSS score ≤ 7 and at least 1 of the following:
   - Persistent signs or symptoms of the ischemic event at the time of randomization,
   - OR Acute, ischemic brain lesion determined by standard-of-care neuroimaging
   - OR Participant underwent thrombolysis or thrombectomy
   - OR TIA: acute onset neurological deficit attributable to focal ischemia of the brain by history or examination, with complete symptom resolution of the deficit and no brain infarction on neuroimaging (eg, CT scan or MRI, performed as part of standard medical practice), and ABCD2 Score ≥6 (p. 39–40)
3. Participants will be randomized as soon as possible after determining eligibility and within 48 hours of onset of event.
   In participants in whom a stroke was evident on awakening from sleep, within 48 hours from the time at which the participants' condition was last reported to be normal.
   Participants who received thrombolysis and/or mechanical thrombectomy with or without stenting can be randomized but only after 24 hours have elapsed after the end of this treatment and not more than 48 hours after the onset of the index event, and provided that post-treatment neuroimaging excludes cerebral bleeding and have post-thrombolytic therapy/post-thrombectomy INR ≤ 1.5 and aPTT ≤ 1.4 times the ULN prior to study randomization.
   Cerebrovascular vessel stenting is allowed as clinically indicated. If these procedures are done pre-randomization, then randomization should occur at least 24 hours after the end of the procedure and not more than 48 hours after the onset of the index event. (p. 40)
4. Current or planned antiplatelet treatment per international and/or local guidelines. If ASA is used, it will be limited to low dose (75 to 100 mg/day). Loading dose of antiplatelet agents (including ASA) are allowed per standard-of-care. (p. 40)

*Weight*: Not applicable (p. 40)

*Sex and Contraceptive/Barrier Requirements*

5. All female participants of childbearing potential must have a negative highly sensitive serum (β-human chorionic gonadotropin [β-hCG]) or urine test at screening (p. 40; nel testo estratto manca il glifo "β")
6. Criterion modified per Amendment 1/EEA-1 — 6.1. A female participant must agree not to be pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant until 4 days (representing >5 half-lives) after the last dose of study intervention (p. 40)
7. A female participant must be (as defined in Appendix 5, Contraceptive and Barrier Guidance) a. Not of childbearing potential or b. Criterion modified per Amendment 1/EEA-1 — 7.b.1. Of childbearing potential and practicing a highly effective method of contraception (failure rate of <1% per year when used consistently and correctly) and agrees to remain on a highly effective method until 4 days (representing >5 half-lives) after last dose of study intervention –the end of relevant exposure. The investigator must evaluate the potential for contraceptive method failure (eg, noncompliance, recently initiated) in relationship to the first dose of study intervention. Examples of highly effective methods of contraception are located in Appendix 5: Contraceptive and Barrier Guidance. (p. 40)
8. Criterion modified per Amendment 1/EEA-1 — 8.1. A female participant using hormonal contraceptives should use an additional non-hormonal contraceptive method (above that required in the inclusion criterion 7b) until 4 days after last dose (representing >5 half-lives) of study intervention –the end of relevant exposure. (p. 40–41)

*Informed Consent*

9. Participant must sign an ICF (or their legally authorized representative must sign) indicating that he or she understands the purpose of, and procedures required for, the study and is willing to participate in the study and willing to provide at least vital status at the end of the study. (p. 41)
10. Willing and able to adhere to the lifestyle restrictions (Section 5.3) specified in this protocol. (p. 41)

> [!note] Struttura del criterio 2
> Il testo mette l'AIT come quarto termine dell'"OR" dentro il criterio "Ischemic Stroke". Il senso, confermato a p. 31 e p. 33, è: (ictus con NIHSS ≤7 e almeno una delle tre condizioni) **oppure** (AIT con ABCD2 ≥6).

### 5.2 Exclusion Criteria (p. 41–42)
"Any potential participant who meets any of the following criteria will be excluded from participating in the study:"

*Medical Conditions*
1. Current intracranial hemorrhage (hemorrhage into the brain, subarachnoid hemorrhage, intraventricular hemorrhage, subdural hematoma or epidural hematoma); except CMB and minor hemorrhagic transformation of infarct manifesting as scattered petechiae (Hemorrhagic Infarction Type 1 [HI1] according to Heidelberg classification). Note: CMBs are defined as rounded foci of <10 mm in size that appear hypointense and are distinct from vascular flow voids, leptomeningeal hemosiderosis, or nonhemorrhagic subcortical mineralization on T2* weighted MRI. (p. 41)
2. Prior history of intracranial hemorrhage except subarachnoid hemorrhage > 1 year prior with adequate treatment. (p. 41)
3. Criterion modified per Amendment 1. 3.1 The index stroke or TIA is considered to have a cardio-embolic etiology based on local standard-of-care investigations and for which guidelines recommend anticoagulation. (p. 41)
4. Criterion modified per Amendment 1. 4.1. The index stroke or TIA considered to have an other known cause, not related to athero-thrombotic sources (TOAST Other Determined Etiology) (Adams 1993), based on local standard-of-care investigations. Examples of such etiologies include intracranial tumor, arteritis, arterial dissection, vasospasm or AVM. (p. 41)
5. Pre-event disability with mRS score of 3 or higher. (p. 41)
6. Any condition that requires chronic anticoagulation at the discretion of the investigator and/or local guidelines. Note: Participants with an indication for chronic antiplatelet therapy after placement of a bio-prosthetic nonmechanical valve (eg, transcatheter aortic valve replacement [TAVR]) do not satisfy this exclusion criterion and are eligible for study participation. (p. 41)
7. Conditions with an increased risk of bleeding, including: Clinically significant bleeding within the previous 3 months; Known bleeding diathesis; Known aPTT prolongation > 1.4 times the ULN or known congenital FXI deficiency; Spinal cord hemorrhage; Retinal hemorrhage (p. 41)
8. Current active liver disease, eg, acute hepatitis, known cirrhosis, including participants receiving antiviral treatment for hepatitis. (p. 42)
9. History of any significant drug allergy (such as anaphylaxis, Stevens-Johnson Syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis, drug reaction with eosinophilia and systemic symptoms [DRESS]). (p. 42)
10. Known allergies, hypersensitivity, or intolerance to milvexian or its excipients (refer to the milvexian IB). (p. 42)
11. Requires dialysis on a permanent basis. (p. 42)
12. eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73 m2 at screening. (p. 42)

*Prior/Concomitant Therapy*

13. Planned use of any disallowed therapies as noted in Section 6.8, Concomitant Therapy including isoniazid. (p. 42)

*Prior/Concurrent Clinical Study Experience*

14. Received an investigational intervention or used an invasive investigational medical device within 4 weeks before the planned first dose of study intervention or is currently enrolled in an investigational interventional study. (p. 42)

*Diagnostic Assessments*

15. Any of the following laboratory results, based on local laboratory, outside of the ranges specified below prior to randomization: ALT 3× ULN; Platelet count <75,000 mm3; Total bilirubin ≥ 1.5x ULN unless an alternative causative factor such as Gilbert's syndrome is identified; Hemoglobin <8.0 g/dL (p. 42; per l'ALT il segno di confronto manca nel testo estratto)

*Other Exclusions*

16. Any condition for which, in the opinion of the investigator, participation would not be in the best interest of the participant (eg, compromise the well-being) or that could prevent, limit, or confound the protocol-specified assessments or has a life expectancy of <12 months. (p. 42)
17. Criterion modified per Amendment 1/EEA-1. 17.1. At the time of screening, any participant who will not consider following the study contact schedule, or not allow a contact to the participant, or to the designated family members (if provided) or health care practitioner, to determine any endpoint events and/or vital status, up until the end of the study. (p. 42)
18. Participants who are incarcerated, including prisoners or participants compulsorily detained for treatment of psychiatric disease. (p. 42)
19. Known current substance abuse that could impact study compliance. (p. 42)
20. Employee of the investigator or study site, with direct involvement in the proposed study or other studies under the direction of that investigator or study site, as well as family members of the employees or the investigator. (p. 42)

"Note: Investigators must ensure that all study enrollment criteria have been met prior to randomization. (…) Waivers are not allowed." (p. 39, p. 42)

## Note e anomalie
- **Il foglio del team non coincide con il protocollo** (dettagli in [[LIBREXIA]]):
  - "TC/RM con ipodensità, deficit di perfusione o occlusione": il protocollo chiede invece una delle tre condizioni (segni persistenti, lesione ischemica acuta, trattamento acuto) e non cita la perfusione né l'occlusione;
  - "prima dose entro 3 h": il protocollo dice "ideally within 2 hours" (p. 44).
- **"non-lacunar"**: era un criterio dello studio di fase 2 AXIOMATIC-SSP (p. 25). Nel fase 3 l'ictus lacunare è ammesso (p. 33).
- **24 h dopo il trattamento**: la nota a della SoA dice "randomization must occur 24 hours after the end of the procedure" (p. 16); il resto del testo dice ">24 hours" (p. 31, p. 39) o "at least 24 hours" (p. 40, p. 49). È un'incoerenza minore.
- **Struttura ambigua del criterio 2**: l'AIT compare come quarto "OR" dentro "Ischemic Stroke" (p. 39–40).
- **Criterio 15**: "ALT 3× ULN" è senza segno di confronto (probabilmente ">" perso nell'estrazione o nell'originale) (p. 42).
- **Metadati del PDF**: "PROT-MHT V6.1 03Aug2020", cioè il template J&J; non è un errore del contenuto.
