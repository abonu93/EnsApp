## Punti chiave
- **Titolo**: "Optimizing Reperfusion to Improve Outcomes and Neurologic Function (ORION): A Multicenter, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Randomized, Parallel-Group, Phase 2/3 Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of JX10 in Acute Ischemic Stroke with Late Presentations" (p. 1). Protocollo JX10002.
- **Promotore**: Corxel Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (New York, USA; sede anche a Shanghai) (p. 1–2). CRO non nominata (p. 91). **Registro**: IND 160639; EU CT 2024-519521-37 (p. 1).
- **Farmaco**: JX10 (già BIIB131/TMS-007), trombolitico che induce un cambio conformazionale del plasminogeno (fibrinolisi "trombo-specifica"), con attività antinfiammatoria (inibizione della sEH) e antiossidante (p. 12, p. 36). Eccipiente HCO-60 (olio di ricino), con rischio noto di anafilassi (p. 38).
- **Disegno**: fase 2/3, multicentrico, in doppio cieco, controllato con placebo, a gruppi paralleli, in due parti (p. 15, p. 42).
  - **Parte 1**: dose-finding, randomizzazione 1:1:1 a JX10 1 mg/kg, 3 mg/kg o placebo, circa 80 per braccio (240) (p. 15, p. 21).
  - **Analisi ad interim** quando è stato arruolato l'ultimo partecipante della Parte 1: l'IDMC raccomanda la dose da portare alla Parte 2; l'accettazione della raccomandazione "will trigger the start of enrollment for Part 2, with site notification" (p. 15, p. 42).
  - **Parte 2**: circa 500 pazienti 1:1 (dose ottimale vs placebo); "Enrollment of Part 2 will start, where permitted, while Part 1 completes the follow-up and database lock". Alcuni centri possono iniziare più tardi per approvazioni regolatorie (p. 15–16, p. 42–43).
  - Totale circa 740 partecipanti, più di 150 centri nel mondo (p. 16, p. 21).
- **Dose**: dose unica in 31 minuti: bolo e.v. del 10% in 1 minuto, poi infusione del 90% in 30 minuti. Massimo 100 mg (1 mg/kg) e 300 mg (3 mg/kg). La dose non si modifica (p. 21, p. 37, p. 52).
- **Finestra**: inizio del farmaco entro **6 h dal LKW senza imaging di perfusione**, oppure entro **24 h con penombra qualificante** (perfusione o altra modalità ammessa) (p. 16, p. 30, p. 43). L'inizio della finestra (4,5 h) può variare secondo le linee guida locali sulla trombolisi (p. 38). L'arruolamento a 16–24 h "may be limited" (p. 43).
- **Trombectomia** (p. 43): "Planned thrombectomy is permitted as per guidelines in the ICA, M1, and proximal M2, if it is performed at least one hour or more, from the beginning of the study drug administration." I centri arruolano solo pazienti che:
  1. non hanno accesso pronto alla EVT;
  2. non sono candidati alla EVT;
  3. per indisponibilità immediata della EVT (es. trasferimento) avranno almeno 1 h fra l'inizio del farmaco e l'inizio della EVT.

  I pazienti con EVT pianificata dopo il farmaco devono essere **non più del 30%** dei randomizzati. Le procedure di salvataggio non pianificate non hanno restrizioni. Nota x della Tab. 1: "allow for an hour between start of study drug infusion and start of endovascular procedure (e.g., femoral puncture)" (p. 30).
- **Imaging di selezione**: TC/RM, angio-TC ± TC-perfusione o angio-RM ± RM-perfusione. I centri senza perfusione (o che preferiscono) possono usare l'angio-TC multifase per il punteggio delle collaterali "instead of target mismatch" (p. 43). Criteri su TC-perfusione: rapporto >1,2, core <70 mL, mismatch >10 mL (p. 44; nei criteri ≥10 mL, vedi Note). La lettura centrale **non** serve per l'arruolamento (p. 91).
- **Randomizzazione**: IRT, stratificata per tipo di ictus (M1, M2 prossimale o ICA vs altri), NIHSS allo screening (>10 vs ≤10) e tempo dall'esordio (4,5–6 h vs >6–12 h vs >12 h) (p. 51).
- **Endpoint primario**: mRS 0–1 a 90 giorni (p. 13, p. 39). **Sicurezza primaria**: sICH (PH2 locale o remoto, ESA e/o EIV entro 36 h dalla randomizzazione con peggioramento NIHSS ≥4, o morte) (p. 14, p. 68).
- **Endpoint secondari**: mRS ordinale, mRS 0–2, Barthel >90, sliding dichotomy, riperfusione e ricanalizzazione a 24 h, TICI 2b/3 nei trattati con EVT, quota di EVT pianificate non eseguite, miglioramento NIHSS >7 o NIHSS 0–1 a 7 giorni (p. 13).
- **Statistica**: Parte 1, 80 per braccio, potenza >90% per OR 3,0 (alfa unilaterale 0,05; mRS 0–1 nel placebo 18,4%, dai dati di fase 2a giapponesi). Parte 2, 500 pazienti, potenza 91% per OR 2,0 (alfa bilaterale 0,05) (p. 21–22, p. 76).
- **Precedenti**: fase 2a giapponese TMS-007-2, entro 12 h: ricanalizzazione 58,3% vs 26,7%; mRS 0–1 a 90 giorni 40,4% vs 18,4%; nessuna sICH con JX10 (p. 34–35).
- **Gestione post-farmaco**: come un paziente post-trombolisi. PA <180/105 mmHg per almeno 24 h; controlli di PA e neurologici ogni 15 min per 2 h, ogni 30 min fino a 6 h, poi ogni ora fino a 24 h (p. 54). Niente antitrombotici (nemmeno a dose profilattica) per 24 h, fino alla TC/RM di controllo; niente altri trombolitici per 48 h (p. 56). Telemetria per almeno 24 h (p. 29, p. 62).
- **Esami pre-dose** (laboratorio locale): emocromo, biochimica (creatinina, eGFR, glucosio), funzione epatica, coagulazione (INR, PT, aPTT, fibrinogeno), test di gravidanza; per lo screening basta la glicemia capillare (p. 27, p. 29, p. 62). ECG a 12 derivazioni allo screening (QTc nei criteri) (p. 26, p. 29). Peso misurato o stimato prima della randomizzazione (p. 29).
- **NIHSS**: da ripetere prima della dose se sono passati ≥60 min dallo screening (p. 30). Si può riselezionare un paziente con NIHSS <5 che peggiora entro il periodo di screening (p. 51).

## Criteri (testuali)
Riportati due volte, identici nella numerazione: sinossi (p. 16–21) e §5.1–5.2 (p. 46–50). Testo dal §5; varianti della sinossi in nota.

### 5.1 Inclusion Criteria (p. 46–47; sinossi p. 16–18)
1. Age ≥ 18 and ≤ 90 years old (Age > 85 must be mRS 0 at baseline (premorbid) to be eligible). (p. 46)
2. Written informed consent by patient, his/her legally authorized representative, or independent physician where local regulation allows (in accordance with all local and national regulations or according to the local ethics committee's guidelines or by another process compliant with applicable national laws and regulations and ethics committee requirements). (p. 46)
3. Acute ischemic stroke with compatible clinical presentation and symptomatic high grade or complete occlusion of the intracranial internal carotid, M1, M2, or distal branches of the middle cerebral artery (MCA), anterior cerebral artery (ACA), or posterior cerebral artery (PCA), demonstrated by: (p. 46)
   a. Computer Tomography Angiography (CTA) or Magnetic Resonance Angiography (MRA) with high grade stenosis or occlusion of an eligible artery, or
   b. Computer Tomography or Magnetic Resonance Perfusion (CTP/MRP) with focal deficit in an eligible artery, or
   c. Non-contrast Computer Tomography (NCCT) with hyperdense artery sign in an eligible artery or Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) with susceptibility vessel sign (defined as a hypointense signal exceeding the diameter of the contralateral artery at the thrombus site) in an eligible artery.
4. Radiographic evidence of salvageable tissue* is present based on: (p. 46)
   a. A mismatch ratio (perfusion lesion (Tmax > 6 seconds) volume/core estimate) > 1.2,
   b. Estimated core infarct volume < 70 mL, and
   c. Total mismatch volume ≥ 10 mL on CTP or magnetic resonance (MR) perfusion-weighted imaging (PWI)/MRI diffusion imaging.

   The perfusion criteria apply to all participants, including those who present within 4.5 to 6 hours of LKW, if perfusion imaging is obtained. Perfusion is mandatory for patients presenting beyond 6 hours, optional for patients < 6 hours.
   \* The quantitative assessment of penumbra may include alternative methods (e.g., if the software uses different parameters in place of Tmax).
5. Presentation with intended study treatment within 4.5 to 6 hours of Last Know Well (LKW) in the absence of radiographic assessment of perfusion or within 4.5 to 24 hours of LKW with radiographic evidence of penumbra meeting criterion (4). (p. 47)
6. Pre-treatment score of NIHSS ≥ 5. (p. 47)
7. Functionally independent prior to stroke onset as evidenced by premorbid mRS < 2 (< 1 if age 86-90). (p. 47)
8. All women of childbearing potential and all men must practice contraception as described in Section 8.3.5. For women of childbearing potential, a negative pregnancy test at screening is required. All women of childbearing potential must practice effective contraception for at least 30 days after their last dose of study treatment. All men must practice effective contraception during the study and for 90 days after their last dose of study treatment. In addition, participants should not donate sperm or eggs during the study and for at least 90 days after their dose of study treatment. (p. 47)

### 5.2 Exclusion Criteria (p. 47–50; sinossi p. 18–21)
*Neurovascular Imaging*
1. Radiographic findings pre-randomization of any of the following: (p. 47)
   a. Large core infarction, evidenced by a core infarct volume > 70 mL, assessed on DWI or CTP; or extensive early ischemic change (hypodensity) on non-contrast CT estimated to be > 1/3 MCA territory, or significant hypodensity outside the Tmax > 6 seconds perfusion lesion that invalidates mismatch criteria, or
   b. Occlusion in more than 1 vascular territory confirmed on CTA/MRA, or
   c. Significant mass effect or clinically significant cerebral edema per Investigator's judgement, or
   d. Evidence of acute intracranial or extracranial hemorrhage, intracranial tumor (except small meningioma), neoplasm, or arteriovenous malformation, or
   e. Clinical history, past imaging, or clinical judgement suggests that the intracranial occlusion is chronic.

*Etiology of Neurologic Symptoms*
2. Non-ischemic or non-thrombotic etiology of current stroke symptoms such as suspected cerebral vasospasm, infectious source (e.g., bacterial endocarditis, septic shock), complications from acute drug abuse (e.g., cocaine intoxication). (p. 47)

*Timing of Endovascular Thrombectomy (EVT)*
3. Availability of endovascular thrombectomy (EVT) for current AIS (in whom EVT is indicated) where planned EVT would be feasible (e.g., time of femoral artery puncture), within 1 hour following the start of study drug infusion. (p. 48)
   - Sinossi: "…within 1 hour following study drug infusion." (senza "the start of") (p. 18)

*Medical History and Current Health Status — Bleeding Risks*
4. Medical history or active clinically significant bleeding, lesions, or conditions (at the investigator's judgement) considered to be of significant risk for major bleeding; (this may include but not limited to any history of intracranial hemorrhage or vascular aneurysm of the large arteries of major intraspinal or intracerebral abnormalities). (p. 48)
5. Cerebral infarction within 90 days of screening. (p. 48)
6. Arterial dissection involving any intracranial artery or the aortic arch. (p. 48)
7. Confirmed acute coronary syndrome within 90 days of screening. (p. 48)
8. Severe hepatic impairment as defined by decompensated liver disease (e.g., Child-Pugh Class C) or clinically significant hepatic condition associated with coagulopathy or bleeding risk. (p. 48)
9. Medical history of chronic renal failure, any condition requiring dialysis or renal replacement therapy or evidence of acute kidney injury at the time of screening, an estimated glomerular filtration rate < 60 mL/min/1.73m2. (p. 48)
10. Severe, uncontrolled hypertension (systolic blood pressure ≥ 185 mmHg or diastolic blood pressure ≥ 110 mmHg) that cannot be controlled with antihypertensive therapy. (p. 48)
11. Known bleeding diathesis (hereditary or acquired) or any significant coagulopathy. Specifically, platelet count < 100,000/μL, international normalized ratio > 1.7, aPTT > 40 seconds, or prothrombin time > 15 seconds. (p. 48)
12. Major trauma, surgery, or invasive procedures: (p. 48–49)
    a. Severe head trauma within 3 months of screening or acute head trauma.
    b. Major trauma not involving the head within 14 days of screening.
    c. Major surgery with 14 days of screening.
    d. Intracranial or intraspinal surgery within 90 days of screening.
    e. Dural puncture (e.g., lumbar puncture) or arterial puncture of a noncompressible blood vessel within 7 days of screening.

*General*
13. Pre-existing medical, neurological, or psychiatric disease that would confound the neurological or functional evaluations of this study. (p. 49)
14. Pre-treatment blood glucose > 400 mg/dL (22.20 mmol/L) or Pre-treatment blood glucose < 50 mg/dL (2.78 mmol/L) unless it is corrected prior to study treatment administration. Participants with subsequently normalized blood glucose levels may be considered for inclusion, per Investigator judgement. (p. 49)
15. Known hereditary or acquired abnormality of UGT1A1 metabolism or deficiency such as Gilbert's syndrome (hereditary liver condition with elevated bilirubin), Crigler-Najjar syndrome (UGT1A1 gene defect associated with congenital non-hemolytic jaundice). (p. 49)
16. Prolonged QT with QTc of > 450 ms for males and > 460 ms for females. (p. 49)
17. Life expectancy less than 6 months due to comorbid condition. (p. 49)

*Medications*
18. Prior thrombolytic administration within 90 days of screening or planned thrombolytic administration for the AIS. (Co-administration with any other thrombolytic agent(s) within 48 hours is prohibited). (p. 49)
19. Known or suspected use of any oral anticoagulant therapy, including but not limited to vitamin K antagonists, thrombin inhibitors, or factor Xa inhibitors, within 48 hours of screening unless blood testing confirms absence of drug substance in the system. (p. 49)
20. Use of dual anti-platelet therapy, IV aspirin, or glycoprotein IIb/IIIa inhibitors within 24 hours of screening. Pre-screening use of oral antiplatelet monotherapy with aspirin at doses less than or equal to 325 mg daily OR clopidogrel 75 mg daily is permitted. (p. 49)
21. Use of heparin or low molecular weight heparin at a therapeutic dose, excluding prescreening prophylactic dose low molecular weight heparin with a normal aPTT. (p. 49)
22. Use of nephrotoxic medications or agents within 7 days of screening that would (in the investigator's opinion) pose significant risk of acute kidney injury (e.g., aminoglycosides, cyclosporins, lactams, amphotericin B, cisplatin, indomethacin, etc.). (p. 49)

*Other Exclusion Criteria*
23. Prior treatment with JX10, any known history of systemic hypersensitivity reaction or anaphylaxis to JX10, the excipients contained in the formulation, and if applicable, any diagnostic agents anticipated to be administered during the study. (p. 50)
24. Participants who are pregnant or currently breastfeeding, and those intending to become pregnant during the study. (p. 50)
25. Current enrollment or a plan to enroll in another investigational device study, or interventional clinical study in which an investigational treatment or approved therapy for investigational use is administered within 90 days prior to the screening. (p. 50)
26. Substance abuse history including heavy alcohol use over the past year, per Investigator judgement, or acute intoxication with alcohol or other substance that may impair participant's ability to comply with treatment and assessment. (p. 50)
27. Inability to comply with study requirements or other unspecified reasons that, in the opinion of the Investigator or Sponsor, make the participant unsuitable for enrollment. (p. 50)

## Consenso (p. 50, p. 88–89)
- Consenso del paziente, del rappresentante legale (LAR) o del medico ("physician per local requirements") prima di qualunque procedura dello studio. Il protocollo incoraggia fax, telefono o telemedicina se la legge e il CEIm lo permettono (p. 50).
- §10.4: il consenso di un'altra persona autorizzata (familiare, caregiver, LAR) si può raccogliere di persona o a distanza, anche con eConsent. Il "emergency consent signed by an independent physician, study investigator, or any other relevant party" è ammesso se lo permette la normativa locale. Il paziente riconferma quando recupera la capacità, se richiesto (p. 88).
- I dati di routine (NIHSS, mRS, imaging, ECG) si possono usare per l'eleggibilità anche se raccolti prima del consenso (p. 50–51).

## Note e anomalie
- **Esclusione 3, tempo della EVT**: il §5.2 misura l'ora "following the start of study drug infusion" (p. 48), come la nota x (p. 30) e il §4.1 (p. 43). La sinossi dice "following study drug infusion" (p. 18), e quindi si può leggere come dalla **fine** dell'infusione.
- **Mismatch**: "≥ 10 mL" nei criteri (p. 17, p. 46) e "> 10 mL" nel §4.1.1 (p. 44).
- **Core a 70 mL**: inclusione "< 70 mL" (p. 46), esclusione "> 70 mL" (p. 47): il valore esatto di 70 mL non è assegnato.
- **mRS per età**: inclusione 1 "Age > 85 must be mRS 0"; inclusione 7 "< 1 if age 86-90". Coerenti fra loro.
- **Vasi**: l'inclusione 3 ammette rami distali di **MCA, ACA e PCA**. La EVT pianificata è ammessa solo per ICA, M1 e M2 prossimale (p. 43).
- **Refusi**: "Last Know Well" (p. 17, p. 47); "pre-stoke mRS" (p. 51); "Major surgery with 14 days" (p. 19, p. 48).
- **Esclusione 9 (eGFR <60)** è molto restrittiva per una popolazione anziana; nella v3.0 diventa <30 (vedi [[ORION — Protocolo — v3.1]]).
