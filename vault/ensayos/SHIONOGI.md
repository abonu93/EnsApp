# SHIONOGI

> [!summary] In breve
> Studio REvive (2138P2231) di Shionogi, fase 2b, randomizzato in doppio cieco contro placebo. Il farmaco è **redasemtide (S-005151)**, un peptide della HMGB1 che mobilizza le cellule staminali mesenchimali. Si somministra in infusione e.v. di 90 min al giorno per 5 giorni, con la prima dose entro 25 h dall'esordio. La popolazione è l'ictus ischemico sopratentoriale con [[NIHSS]] 8–22 stabile, [[mRS]] pre 0–1 e **nessuna indicazione** a trombolisi o trombectomia ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 1, p. 18, p. 30–31). Il CI del 2025 aggiunge una coorte B con pazienti trombolisati o trombectomizzati ([[SHIONOGI — CI — v7.1]], p. 2).

> [!danger] La voce dell'app mescola tre studi
> `trials-info.ts:180-197` (chiave `Shionogi`) descrive come intervento **"Elezanumab 1800 mg IV vs placebo (120 min infusion)"** (`:191`). È il farmaco di **ABBVIE** nel foglio del team, uno studio non ancora aperto. Anche l'imaging "CT or MRI post-thrombolysis to exclude ICH; MRI < 4 days after" (`:187`) è la riga di ABBVIE del foglio ("TC o RM post-TM per escludere HIC; RM <4 giorni", [[Esquema ensayos — septiembre 2026]]). La nota "If TEV/stent is planned, wait > 24 h after study drug" (`:188`, `:192`) è invece la regola di LIBREXIA ("TEV/stent: attendere >24 h"). Della vera SHIONOGI restano solo la finestra di 25 h, il mRS 0–1, l'età adulta e le visite a 30 ± 7 e 90 (−7/+14) giorni (`:185`, `:193-196`). Dettagli nelle divergenze.

## Disegno
- **Tipo**: fase 2b, multinazionale, multicentrico, randomizzato 1:1:1, doppio cieco, controllato con placebo, a gruppi paralleli ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 11, p. 26).
- **Bracci (protocollo v2.0)**: redasemtide 0,75 mg/kg, redasemtide 1,5 mg/kg, placebo ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 12).
- **Bracci (CI v7.1)**: redasemtide 1,5 mg/kg o placebo, 50% ciascuno; coorte A (non candidati a riperfusione) e coorte B (candidati o già trattati con trombolisi e/o trombectomia); circa 679 pazienti ([[SHIONOGI — CI — v7.1]], p. 2–3, p. 14).
- **Randomizzazione**: centrale via IRT, stratificata per regione, NIHSS (≤12/≥13), età (18–74/≥75) e tempo dall'esordio (<18/≥18 h) ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 35).
- **Numerosità (v2.0)**: 564 valutabili, circa 627 arruolati ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 12).
- **Endpoint primario**: mRS a 90 giorni (ordinale). Secondari chiave: mRS 0–2 e Barthel ≥95 a 90 giorni ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 10).

## Criteri di inclusione
Testo originale (§5.1, [[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 30–31):

1. Male or female adults ≥ 18 years of age (or complies with country-specific regulatory requirements) at the time of obtaining informed consent (p. 30)
2. Capable of giving signed informed consent as described in the ICF that includes compliance with the requirements and restrictions listed in the ICF and in this protocol. If the participant cannot provide consent, consent from the participant's proxy/legally authorized representative (LAR) is allowed according to local regulations. (p. 30)
3. Diagnosis of supratentorial (including anterior and posterior circulation) ischemic stroke as the main infarction site, confirmed by computed tomography (CT) scan or magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) at the time of Screening. The CT imaging criteria are hypodensity, perfusion deficit, or vessel occlusion in the anterior or posterior circulation. (p. 30)
4. Able to initiate study intervention within 25 hours of stroke onset (if the onset time is unknown, the last time at which the participant was known to be healthy is regarded as the onset time ["last known to be well"]) (p. 30)
5. Determined by the investigator to not be eligible for recanalization thrombolysis or endovascular recanalization therapy (ie, mechanical thrombectomy, local fibrinolytic therapy) for the current stroke. (Note: Participants who underwent mechanical thrombectomy, but a stent could not be placed to reach the occluded vessel site may be eligible for this study.) Please refer to the most recent tPA package insert for contraindications. (p. 30)
6. Baseline NIHSS score of 8 to 22 (inclusive) and stable, defined as absence of an increase or decrease of ≥ 4 points within ≥ 1 hour to ≤ 3 hours between Screening and baseline assessment of NIHSS score (p. 30)
7. Medically stable at the time of enrollment except for primary disease and complications associated with it, according to the judgment of the investigator. In addition, hospitalization during the Follow-up Period is not anticipated, and the participant appears likely to be able to complete the study. ▪ Medically stable is defined as disease not requiring significant change in therapy for 3 months following enrollment (p. 30)
8. Body weight of ≥ 30 to ≤ 150 kg (p. 31)
9. A female participant is eligible to participate if she is not pregnant or breastfeeding, and 1 of the following conditions applies: WONCBP […] OR WOCBP and using an acceptable contraceptive method […] during the Treatment Period. […] negative highly sensitive pregnancy test […] before the first dose of study intervention. (p. 31)

**Sintesi**: adulto, ictus ischemico sopratentoriale confermato in TC o RM, NIHSS 8–22 stabile, nessuna indicazione a trombolisi o trombectomia, prima dose entro 25 h, peso 30–150 kg, test di gravidanza negativo se fertile.

## Criteri di esclusione
Testo originale (§5.2, [[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 31–32):

1. Any disease or neurological disorder that, in the opinion of the investigator, would interfere with the conduct of the study (p. 31)
2. A severe decrease in consciousness level (defined as NIHSS item 1a score 3: Not alert, responds only with reflex motor or autonomic effects, or totally unresponsive, flaccid, and areflexic) (p. 31)
3. Disability corresponding to a mRS score of ≥ 2 before the onset of stroke (p. 31)
4. A history of stroke (excluding transient ischemic attack), history of or current intracranial hemorrhage, or head trauma that caused neurological effects within 90 days prior to obtaining informed consent (p. 31)
5. Participants with an ischemic stroke in cerebellum and/or brain stem as the main infarction site (p. 31)
6. Diagnosis of a current transient ischemic attack (p. 32)
7. Unable to undergo either CT or MRI (p. 32)
8. Considered by the investigator to be inappropriate to participate due to a history or complication of serious cardiovascular disease within 1 month of Screening (eg, history of acute myocardial infarction, current acute myocardial infarction, uncontrollable heart failure, infective endocarditis requiring treatment, or acute aortic dissection, or requiring or likely to require hospitalization for severe arrhythmia during the study) (p. 32)
9. Blood glucose level < 50 or > 400 mg/dL after glycemic control (p. 32)
10. Systolic blood pressure ≥ 220 mm Hg or diastolic blood pressure ≥ 120 mm Hg after antihypertensive treatment (p. 32)
11. Current or ongoing anticancer treatment; however, nonmelanoma skin cancer or lesions equivalent to intramucosal cancer or carcinoma in situ curable by endoscopic resection may be eligible for this study (p. 32)
12. Sensitivity to any of the study interventions, or components thereof, or clinically significant drug or other severe allergy that, in the opinion of the investigator, contraindicates participation in the study (p. 32)
13. Use of prohibited concomitant medications or therapies listed in Section 6.8 for the treatment of current AIS (p. 32)
14. Participants who have previously received redasemtide (p. 32)
15. Participants who have received any investigational product within 90 days of Screening (p. 32)

**Sintesi**: esclusi coma (1a = 3), mRS pre ≥2, ictus/emorragia/trauma cranico nei 90 giorni precedenti, infarto principale in cervelletto o tronco, AIT, eventi cardiovascolari gravi nell'ultimo mese, glicemia <50 o >400, PA ≥220/120 dopo trattamento, terapia antitumorale in corso, altri farmaci sperimentali nei 90 giorni.

## Intervento e procedure
- **Farmaco**: redasemtide 0,75 o 1,5 mg/kg (CI v7.1: solo 1,5 mg/kg) o placebo, infusione e.v. di 90 min (±15) una volta al giorno per 5 giorni consecutivi, in ricovero obbligatorio. Prima dose entro 25 h dall'esordio ed entro 90 min dalla randomizzazione; seconda dose 12–30 h dopo la prima; poi ogni 24 h (±1) ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 18, p. 26, p. 34).
- **Trombolisi e trombectomia**: nel protocollo v2.0 sono **criteri di esclusione** per l'ictus indice e **vietate** dal consenso alla fine dello studio (salvo complicanze vascolari o un nuovo ictus). Ammesso chi ha avuto una trombectomia in cui lo stent non ha raggiunto l'occlusione ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 30, p. 37–38). Le terapie approvate non vanno mai ritardate per rendere eleggibile il paziente (p. 11, p. 27). Il CI v7.1 introduce la coorte B con pazienti già trombolisati o trombectomizzati ([[SHIONOGI — CI — v7.1]], p. 2).
- **Esami pre-dose**: ematologia, biochimica, coagulazione (APTT, PT-INR), urine, HBV/HCV/HIV, test di gravidanza; peso misurato prima dell'arruolamento ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 15–17, p. 68).

## Consenso
- **Tipo**: scritto, del paziente o del rappresentante legale ("proxy/LAR") secondo la normativa locale ([[SHIONOGI — Protocolo — v2.0 (Enmienda 1)]], p. 30, p. 63).
- **CI v7.1**: firma del paziente, o del rappresentante legale, familiare o persona vincolata di fatto, con firma successiva del paziente; testimone imparziale se necessario ([[SHIONOGI — CI — v7.1]], p. 2, p. 15–16).
- Consenso orale o telefonico: non previsto. Lingue in Drive: solo castellano.

## Co-arruolamento

## Problemi documentali

- **Archiviazione** (segnalazione dell'inventario, non verificata in questa sessione): una copia identica del protocollo vigente era archiviata per errore in "Protocolo obsoleto"; la cartella originale è in CERRADOS ma ha attività fino ad agosto 2026.
- **Stato**: lo studio non compare nel foglio [[Esquema ensayos — septiembre 2026]]; l'app lo marca `closed`, coerente con l'indice Drive.
- **Voce dell'app ibrida** (vedi sopra): prima di usare la chiave `Shionogi` come base per ABBVIE o di riaprirla, va riscritta da zero.
